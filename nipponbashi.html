A language school named after a bridge, because that's exactly what learning one is.

NipponBashi was founded in 2011 by a small group of translators and teachers who kept noticing the same thing: students didn't struggle with grammar tables, they struggled with the gap between "knowing" Japanese and "using" it.

So we built our curriculum like a bridge — short, well-anchored spans instead of one long leap. Every level connects directly to the next, every lesson is grounded in conversation you'll actually have, and every teacher is trained to walk beside you, not just ahead of you.

教え方 — Our method

Three ideas hold up everything we teach

Short, connected spans

Each lesson closes a small, specific gap and links cleanly to the next, so progress never feels like guesswork.

Teachers who stay in step

Every class is led by a bilingual instructor trained to notice where you're stuck and slow down without breaking momentum.

Conversation over memorisation

Grammar is taught in service of speaking, never as an end in itself — you'll be using each lesson within the same week.
